# That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime

![Rimuru Tempest](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/2/2b/That_Time_I_Got_Reincarnated_as_a_Slime_light_novel_volume_1_cover.jpg)

## 📖 Overview
**That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime** (Tensei Shitara Slime Datta Ken) is a Japanese anime and light novel series written by *Fuse*.  
It tells the story of Satoru Mikami, an ordinary office worker who is reincarnated into a fantasy world as a slime monster named **Rimuru Tempest**.

## 🧙‍♂️ Main Characters
- Rimuru Tempest  
- Shizu  
- Benimaru  
- Shion  
- Milim Nava  
- Veldora Tempest  

## ⚔️ Plot Summary
After being killed in the real world, Satoru awakens in another world as a slime with special abilities.  
He befriends monsters, gains powerful allies, and eventually builds a peaceful nation called **Tempest**, where humans and monsters coexist.

## 💬 Why I Like It
> I really like this series because of its mix of adventure, action, and humor.  
> Rimuru’s calm but powerful personality and his goal to create a fair world make him one of my favorite anime protagonists.

## 🌟 Favorite Episode
**Season 2, Episode 11 – Birth of a Demon Lord**  
This episode shows Rimuru’s most powerful moment when he uses **Megiddo**, a devastating rain of light that destroys his enemies.  
It’s one of the most intense and emotional scenes in the series.

## 🧩 Fun Fact
The anime was adapted from a web novel and light novel series.  
Because of its popularity, it now has multiple seasons and even a movie.
